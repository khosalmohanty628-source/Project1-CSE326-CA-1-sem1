Welcome to Anandpur Sahib

Anandpur Sahib (meaning “City of Bliss”) is one of the most sacred places in Sikhism. It was founded in 1665 by Guru Tegh Bahadur, the ninth Sikh Guru, near the village of Makhowal in present-day Punjab. The settlement was originally called Chakk Nanaki, named after Guru Tegh Bahadur's mother, Mata Nanaki. The city became the main center of Sikh activities under Guru Gobind Singh, the tenth Sikh Guru. In 1699, on the festival of Vaisakhi, Guru Gobind Singh established the Khalsa Panth at Anandpur Sahib, a landmark event in Sikh history. Anandpur Sahib is also home to Takht Sri Kesgarh Sahib, one of the five Takhts in Sikhism, and is famous worldwide for the vibrant annual Hola Mohalla festival.
