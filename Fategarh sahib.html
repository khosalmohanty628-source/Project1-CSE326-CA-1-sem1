Welcome to Fatehgarh Sahib

Fatehgarh Sahib is a historic city of immense spiritual and cultural importance. The name "Fatehgarh" translates to "Town of Victory", named in honor of the triumph over oppression. It is globally revered as the site where Baba Zorawar Singh Ji and Baba Fateh Singh Ji, the young sons (Chhote Sahibzade) of the tenth Sikh Guru, Guru Gobind Singh Ji, made the supreme sacrifice in 1704 rather than renounce their faith and values.

The city is also home to Aam Khas Bagh, a historic Mughal royal garden built during the reigns of Akbar and Shah Jahan, and the ancient archaeological site of Sanghol (Ucha Pind) showcasing relics from the Harappan and Kushan civilizations.
