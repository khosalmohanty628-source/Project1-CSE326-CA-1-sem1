Punjab Travel Guide

Welcome to the Punjab Travel Guide, your ultimate digital gateway to exploring the vibrant heart of northern India. Known across the world as the "Land of Five Rivers," Punjab is a captivating destination where ancient history, deep spirituality, and unmatched hospitality come together. We created this platform to help travelers, students, and culture enthusiasts discover the true essence of Punjabiyat. From the peaceful golden reflections of Sri Harmandir Sahib in Amritsar and the historic echoes of Jallianwala Bagh to the royal architecture of Patiala and the modern charm of Chandigarh, our guide covers every must-visit landmark. Beyond sightseeing, we take you on a sensory journey through bustling local bazaars, colorful festivals like Baisakhi and Lohri, and the rich flavors of authentic Punjabi cuisine. Whether you are planning a weekend getaway or a deep cultural dive, our guide offers everything you need for an unforgettable journey.
