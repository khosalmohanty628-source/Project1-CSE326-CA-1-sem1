Welcome to Amritsar

Amritsar is one of Punjab's oldest and most historic cities. It was founded in 1577 by the fourth Sikh Guru, Guru Ram Das Ji, who established the city around the sacred Amrit Sarovar (Pool of Nectar). The city later became the spiritual and cultural center of Sikhism with the construction of the Golden Temple. Today, Amritsar is known worldwide for its rich heritage, religious significance, and the historic Jallianwala Bagh memorial.
