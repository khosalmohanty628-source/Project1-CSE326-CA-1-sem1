Welcome to Ropar

Ropar (officially known as Rupnagar) is an ancient city located on the banks of the Sutlej River. It is one of the most significant archaeological and historical destinations in northern India, being the first Harappan (Indus Valley) civilization site excavated in independent India, with relics dating back to 2500 BCE. In 1831, Ropar was the historic venue where Maharaja Ranjit Singh and British Governor-General Lord William Bentinck signed the Treaty of Amritsar. Today, Ropar is also famous for the protected Ropar Wetland (a Ramsar site that hosts thousands of migratory birds) and sacred gurdwaras such as Gurudwara Sri Bhatha Sahib.
