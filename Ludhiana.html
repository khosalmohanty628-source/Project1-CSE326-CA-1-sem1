Welcome to Ludhiana

Ludhiana is Punjab's largest and most bustling city, affectionately known as the "Manchester of India" for its world-leading textile, wool, and bicycle manufacturing industries. Founded in 1480 by the Lodhi dynasty, the city sits near an old bed of the Sutlej River. Today, Ludhiana seamlessly blends rich industrial energy with educational excellence at Punjab Agricultural University (the birthplace of the Green Revolution in India), patriotic history at Maharaja Ranjit Singh War Museum, and bustling markets surrounding the historic Clock Tower.
