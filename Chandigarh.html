Welcome to Chandigarh

Chandigarh is India's first planned city, designed by the famous architect Le Corbusier after independence. It serves as the capital of both Punjab and Haryana and is known for its modern architecture, wide roads, beautiful gardens, and clean urban environment. Today, Chandigarh is one of India's best-planned and greenest cities.
