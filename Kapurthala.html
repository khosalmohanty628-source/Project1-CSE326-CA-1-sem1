Welcome to Kapurthala

Kapurthala, often referred to as the "Paris of Punjab", was the capital of the erstwhile Kapurthala princely state. The city is renowned for its extraordinary architectural blending of French neoclassical and Indo-Saracenic styles, commissioned by Maharaja Jagatjit Singh. Highlights include the magnificent Jagatjit Palace (modeled after the Palace of Versailles), the rare Moorish Mosque inspired by the Grand Mosque of Marrakech, the historic Shalimar Gardens, and the unique Panch Mandir temple complex.
