Welcome to Patiala

Patiala is the royal heart of Punjab, famous worldwide for its opulent heritage, classical music traditions (Patiala Gharana), fine culinary legacy, and distinctive royal craftsmanship—from the iconic Patiala Shahi turban and salwar suits to handcrafted leather juttis. Founded in 1763 by Baba Ala Singh, the city is crowned by the magnificent 10-acre Qila Mubarak palace complex, the shimmering Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors), the majestic Moti Bagh Palace, and the sprawling botanical Baradari Gardens.
